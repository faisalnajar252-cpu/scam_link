A few observations

Collected without pretending to have everything figured out.

OBSERVATION 01

Conversation is not a transaction

The best conversations do not always have a destination. Sometimes the conversation itself becomes the destination.

OBSERVATION 02

Small things become memorable

A phrase, a pause, a joke or an ordinary reply can become strangely memorable when it comes from the right person.

OBSERVATION 03

There is no neat conclusion

Perhaps that is the point. Some stories are better left slightly unfinished, with another conversation waiting somewhere ahead.

The simplest description

Two people. One growing sense of familiarity.

Not a perfect story. Not a carefully planned one. Just two lives crossing paths, sharing thoughts, finding humour in small things, and gradually making an ordinary connection feel a little less ordinary.
