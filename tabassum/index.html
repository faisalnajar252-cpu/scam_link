What this website has learned

After extensive and completely unqualified research.

FINDING A

Conversation ≠ efficiency

Sometimes the point isn't reaching the conclusion. Sometimes the point is taking the scenic route through 47 unrelated topics.

FINDING B

Small messages matter

A short reply can carry more personality than a paragraph. Especially when it contains exactly one “thk”.

FINDING C

There is always more to say

Even after “good night”, somehow another topic appears. This is apparently a feature, not a bug.
