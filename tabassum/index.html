This project began with a few ordinary conversations and quickly became a problem that could not be solved with logic alone. So naturally, it required a website.

The hypothesis

Can two people have a completely normal conversation that is, statistically speaking, far less normal than it appears?

02 / Observations

Unexpected findings

After extensive field research, absolutely no funding, and an unreasonable amount of thought.

OBS_001

The “thk hai” phenomenon

Two words. Multiple meanings. Tone-dependent interpretation. A surprisingly high impact-to-character ratio.

OBS_002

Conversation latency

Waiting for a reply appears to distort the perception of time. Further research is required. Obviously.

OBS_003

Unexpected depth

Some perfectly ordinary sentences suddenly become philosophical when you read them twice at 2 a.m.

05 / Final report

The data does not support a completely ordinary explanation.

There is no grand scientific conclusion. Just a pattern: conversations keep happening, small things keep becoming memorable, and somehow the person on the other side of the screen becomes a little harder to describe as “just someone”.
